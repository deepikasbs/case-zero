CASE CLOSED

The truth behind the theft

The thief was Dr. Evelyn Shaw, the museum's restoration specialist. During the gala, she used her authorized access to quietly replace the Star of Vesper with a convincing replica she had prepared in advance. The intact display and silent alarm weren't signs of a break-in—they were the clues that someone with legitimate access had planned the switch.

Her timeline placed her alone in the restoration wing, but the maintenance log showed the wing was locked before she claimed to arrive. The blue fiber found on the display matched the lining of her restoration case. The case wasn't forced; it was used as cover.

Detective's note: The strongest case comes from combining access, timeline contradictions, and physical evidence—not from any single clue.
